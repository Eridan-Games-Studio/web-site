Which Eridan World Interests You Most?

You can choose one, two or three options, in order of importance. From most to least.

Atomic Horizon

Retrofuturist Solar System

A retrofuturist Solar System at the end of the 21st century, where corporations rule colonies and Earth lies quarantined. Space is not adventure but a workplace: fusion reactors power expansion, analog technology prevents AI hijacking, and exosuit operators wage wars over fuel and territory in the hard vacuum.

H.A.V.E.N

Supernatural Noir

A supernatural-noir city where neon lights hum with paranormal energy, blurring the line between technology and the occult. Navigate webs of power, corruption, and forbidden knowledge as the city watches your steps, listens to your whispers, and bends events towards chaos.

Rule of Rika

Mythic Space Opera

A mythic space opera where the planetoid Rika serves as judge, jury, and executioner for the galaxy. Alien civilizations—each shaped by unique biologies, cultures and technologies—are placed into a cycle of sanctioned conflict and high-stakes cooperation. In Rika's shadow, every victory is a step toward galactic ascendancy.
