A Rule of Rika story

Rite of Rika

Where wars that could consume worlds are reduced to two intertwined struggles: the clash of champions below, and the contest of influence above.

Approach the altar
I

The Arbiter

A judgment beyond war

In this Galactic Cycle, disputes that could devastate worlds are brought before Rika—a sentient planetoid whose authority reaches into the workings of space and time. Territorial claims, economic conflicts, political alliances, and contested histories find their resolution through two intertwined struggles: the clash of champions on her surface and the contest of influence in the Council above.

Among the seven major races seeking their place in this cycle, four stand at the centre of Rite of Rika: the Akvira Empire, Liguni Confluence, Dindrae Tetrarky, and Zo Har Unity. Each arrives with its own ambitions, its own understanding of justice, and its own means of securing both.

Some come willingly, undertaking the Pilgrimage to submit their cause. Others answer the Call, summoned when Rika judges that their affairs require her intervention. Before her altars, the disputing parties declare their claims. Rika appoints the place and time at which strength of arms and strength of influence will determine a binding resolution.

II

The Two Who Act as One

Chancellor & General

Each side entrusts its cause to two figures: a Chancellor and a General, joined through a mind link.

◇

The Council · Above

The Chancellor

The Chancellor leads a delegation into the Galactic Council, housed within a crystalline station fashioned by Rika herself and suspended in orbit around her. Here, opposing delegations present evidence, contest interpretations, bargain, threaten, and undermine one another’s standing.

An exposed mining operation on another race’s world may weaken a territorial claim; a carefully timed intervention may turn the attending delegates against a rival. Matters of galactic importance draw powerful witnesses, while lesser disputes may attract few observers—or none. Rika watches over them all.

The player acting as Chancellor directs senators and other members of the delegation through cards, choosing when to advance an argument, reveal information, employ a tactic, spy, or sabotage. The struggle is for influence in the Council, but its effects can reach far beyond the chamber.

Mind link
⬙

The Battlefield · Below

The General

Below, the General commands a chosen company of heroes and units on Rika’s shifting terrain. These champions bear the weight of conflicts that might otherwise consume armies and civilian worlds.

Their battles belong to the ancient tradition of trial by combat, in which selected warriors stand for the people and claims behind them. The General must bring their distinct abilities into concert, manoeuvring, striking, and protecting those whose survival may decide the encounter.

One intention, two arenas

The mind link makes these two struggles a shared undertaking.

At the beginning of each round, the Chancellor shows the General two cards, expressing through play the possibilities communicated between their minds. Across the distance between Council and battlefield, political intent becomes a tactical opportunity.

An Akvira Chancellor may arrange the delivery of equipment to the fighting below; a Liguni Chancellor may intrude upon an enemy’s mind. Other interventions may enable movement, protect a threatened unit, or disrupt an opponent’s plans.

Such powers operate within possibilities that Rika has already approved.

III

Sanctioned Possibility

What Rika permits

Before the contest, each delegation places data crystals upon her altars, submitting its proposed combatants, resources, and interventions. Rika’s acceptance establishes what may be brought into that particular dispute.

◆

At the table

In play, this preparation takes the form of drafting a deck and selecting a force from a larger pool of cards and units. Choosing them together allows the players to build a strategy in which the Chancellor’s interventions support the General’s company.

A card therefore represents an authorised possibility. When the Chancellor invokes it, Rika permits its effect to enter the encounter. Some effects take the familiar form of testimony, sabotage, or delivered equipment. Others appear almost magical: a warrior gains an extraordinary opportunity to move, a defence manifests, or a Liguni unit is forced to decouple. The Chancellor can exercise this limited power over events because Rika has sanctioned it.

A Million Judgments

One place.
Countless realities.

Nor is Rika bound to host these contests one after another. By shifting her own phase in reality, she can sustain thousands—even millions—of encounters within the same region of space. Each unfolds within a distinct pocket of reality, with its own battlefield and corresponding Council proceedings.

Those present experience only the few contests belonging to their instance, while Rika presides over them all.

The resolution awaits

Make your preparations count.

Within each encounter, the Chancellor and General must make their preparations count. The Chancellor works to erode the opposing delegation’s influence while opening possibilities for the battlefield. The General turns those possibilities into action.

Together, they seek a resolution that will carry beyond Rika and reshape their people’s place in the galaxy.
